Justice Fast RVA

We’re once again partnering with Justice Fast RVA for a post-event fast. Funds collected during the One Day, One Step 2026 Justice Fast benefit organizations fighting food insecurity in our area. When you register for One Day, One Step, you’ll have the option to sign up for the fast — together, we continue to pray and fast for justice and healing in our city.
